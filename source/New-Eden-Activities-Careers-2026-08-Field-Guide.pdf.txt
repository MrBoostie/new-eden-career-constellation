Field guide: activity summaries, starting points, risks and current primary references
PVE
ROOKIE & MISSION PVE
Career agents • Level 1–5 security missions • Epic arcs • Homefront Operations • Exordium starter activities
Learn ships, navigation, fitting and combat in low-risk content. Exordium is PvP-free but pays less than normal highsec.
https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy/careers/enforcer
EXPLORATION
Probe scanning • Data & relic sites • Ghost & Sleeper caches • DED sites & escalations • Wormhole mapping
Scan signatures and hack containers. Value and danger rise sharply outside highsec; always plan the exit first.
https://www.eveonline.com/eve-academy/careers/explorer
WORMHOLE PVE
Sleeper anomalies • Gas harvesting • Blue-loot farming • Rampant Drone Fabricator • Nomadic living
No local intelligence, shifting routes and polarized holes make preparation part of the activity, not paperwork.
https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Wormholes
ABYSS & COMBAT SITES
Abyssal Deadspace (T0–T6) • Combat anomalies • Pirate strongholds / FOBs • Incursions • Limited-time event sites
Abyssal Deadspace is timed filament PvE with weather, tiers and severe loss risk — not agent mission running.
https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Abyssal_Deadspace
EMPIRE CAMPAIGNS
Military Campaign objectives • Seasonal mining & hacking • Campaign hauling / industry • Faction standings & LP • Visions of Greatness epic arc
Cradle of War campaigns combine PvE, PvP, mining, hacking, hauling and industry with lasting faction outcomes.
https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/cradle-of-war-in-focus

FLEET WARFARE
Factional Warfare • Pirate insurgencies • Nullsec sovereignty war • Structure warfare • Capital & command fleets

AMBUSH & DENIAL
Gate camps • Bombing & interdiction • Combat probing • Black-ops drops • ESS reserve-bank robbery

HIGHSEC CONFLICT
War declarations • Suicide ganking • Suspect baiting • Kill-right hunting • Anti-ganking / escort

Skyhook theft • Mercenary Den attacks • Metenox raids • POCO conflict • Supply-line interdiction

INDUSTRY
RESOURCE HARVESTING
Ore & ice mining • Gas harvesting • Moon extraction • Planetary industry • Salvage
Mine ore, ice, gas and moons; extract PI; salvage battlefields. Fleet boosts and compression shape scale.

2026 MINING
Built-in mining surveyor • Mining critical successes • Phased asteroid fields • Erratic ore reactions • Map-based ore scouting
Catalyst added surveying overlays, faster cycles, critical successes, phased fields and variable erratic ores.
https://www.eveonline.com/news/view/catalyst-expansion-notes
MANUFACTURING
T1 production • T2 invention & production • T3 reverse engineering • Capital construction • Structure production
Turn blueprints and components into ships, modules and structures. Margin begins with cost accounting.
https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Manufacturing
REACTIONS & PROCESSING
Reprocessing • Composite reactions • Biochemical reactions • Hybrid / polymer reactions • Unrefined mineral reactions
Refine raw resources and run structure-based reactions for advanced materials and production chains.
https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Reactions
TRADE & LOGISTICS
Station & regional trading • Hauling / courier contracts • Jump-freighter logistics • Industry supply chains • Market speculation
Move goods or information across regional markets. Collateral, route security and fees decide profitability.
https://wiki.eveuniversity.org/Trading
