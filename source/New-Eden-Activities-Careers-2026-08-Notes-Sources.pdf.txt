NEW EDEN ACTIVITIES & CAREERS EDITION 2026.08 • VERIFIED 19 AUGUST 2026
Method, major changes since the 2023 reference, limitations and primary sources
WHAT THIS IS
A new, independently designed August 2026 guide. It uses the supplied 2023 chart only as background research, then rebuilds the taxonomy, wording, links and visual system from scratch.
WHY IT WAS REBUILT, NOT EDITED
The supplied chart states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. The NoDerivatives term allows unchanged sharing but not distribution of a modified chart. These files therefore do not alter, republish or masquerade as a new version of that work.
MAJOR 2024–2026 ADDITIONS
Equinox sovereignty, Orbital Skyhooks, Metenox Moon Drills, Mercenary Dens and MTOs, Freelance Jobs, corporation Projects, Pirate Insurgencies, modern Factional Warfare, Homefront Operations, SKINR, Catalyst mining, Exordium, Military Campaigns, achievements/titles and Visions of Greatness.
MAJOR CORRECTIONS
Abyssal Deadspace is described as timed filament PvE rather than missions. Wormhole settlement uses Upwell structures rather than POS-era language. Moon work distinguishes active refinery extraction from automated Metenox drilling. Project Discovery now focuses on cancer and immune-system research.
DESIGN LIMITS
Difficulty, cost, account eligibility, profitability and risk change with skills, location, balance patches, player behavior and market conditions. This edition intentionally avoids pretending they fit into immutable one-to-three chevrons. Verify ship and clone requirements in-game.
LINK POLICY
Cards point to official CCP pages when available and EVE University for durable mechanics references. No URL shorteners, archived forums, generic search pages, dead third-party blogs or unexplained YouTube links are used.
